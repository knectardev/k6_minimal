Background – Massachusetts Municipal Wholesale Electric Company (MMWEC)
The Massachusetts Municipal Wholesale Electric Company (MMWEC) is the Commonwealth's designated joint‑action agency for municipal utilities. An article about a recent power‑purchase agreement notes that MMWEC is a not‑for‑profit, public corporation and political subdivision of Massachusetts created in 1975 to help municipal utilities collaborate for low‑cost, reliable and clean energy.

MMWEC's statute allows the agency to issue tax‑exempt debt to finance energy facilities and enter agreements that reduce costs for member utilities. Beyond financing, MMWEC provides member utilities with power‑supply, financial and risk‑management services. These services support 23 municipal light departments and help them meet clean‑energy mandates.

MMWEC also runs NextZero, an energy‑efficiency program for residential, commercial and industrial customers of participating municipal utilities. According to a program summary, NextZero offers home energy audits, appliance and lighting rebates, air‑conditioner and heat‑pump rebates and other incentives, with participating utilities tailoring the suite of rebates for their customers.

Challenge
MMWEC's member utilities and energy‑efficiency programs generate large volumes of data, documents and customer interactions. Before this project, MMWEC lacked a modern platform for member collaboration, content management, and energy-efficiency rebate processing.

Solution
Working with MMWEC, we designed and implemented a comprehensive digital platform including a secure member portal and a modern rebate application system. The solution streamlined collaboration, improved transparency, and enhanced user experience across all touchpoints.

Impact
The new platform centralized information access, improved operational efficiency, and enhanced energy-efficiency program engagement. By digitizing manual processes and implementing granular user management, MMWEC gained a scalable foundation for supporting their municipal utility members and accelerating Massachusetts' transition toward clean energy.
