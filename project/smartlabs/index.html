SmartLabs Brochure & Member/Vivarium Portals

Overview
SmartLabs transforms how biotech companies approach facilities by providing turnkey lab infrastructure—from wet labs to vivariums and small-scale manufacturing. This allows research teams to focus on discovery rather than managing real estate and operations. As SmartLabs expanded across Boston, Cambridge, and San Francisco, they needed a unified digital ecosystem that could scale with their physical growth.

The Challenge: Create a comprehensive platform spanning public marketing, secure member operations, and specialized compliance management—all while maintaining enterprise-grade security and seamless integrations with existing tools.

The Solution: A three-tier digital ecosystem comprising a modern marketing site, a multi-tenant member portal, and a specialized vivarium compliance platform.

The Challenge
SmartLabs faced a complex digital infrastructure challenge that spanned both marketing and mission-critical operations:

Multi-Tenant Complexity

  • Each client organization needed private workspace within a shared platform
  • SmartLabs administrators required cross-tenant visibility
  • Enterprise-grade identity management through SSO providers (Okta, OneLogin, Azure)
  • Authoritative user data via cloud LDAP integration

Integration Requirements
The platform needed to connect with SmartLabs' operational backbone:

  • BioRAFT for safety compliance
  • TEEM for room scheduling
  • Zendesk for support ticketing
  • HubSpot/Salesforce for CRM
  • WiCom for vivarium hardware telemetry

Compliance & Security

  • Digital IACUC/IBC form workflows
  • Enforced badge limits per company for labs and vivariums
  • Physical access system integration
  • AAALAC compliance for animal research facilities

Our Approach

1. Marketing Foundation
We rebuilt the public-facing site as a modern, responsive platform that empowers non-technical staff to publish content quickly and consistently. The new site features:

  • Clear Value Proposition: Program and location pages that articulate the SmartLabs model of enterprise-grade labs with expert guidance
  • Interactive Experience: 360° facility tours and interactive modules that demonstrate "turnkey" operations
  • Social Proof: Structured case study templates highlighting client outcomes
  • Seamless Transition: Global login routing that connects prospects to the secure portal without friction

2. Multi-Tenant Member Portal
Built on Drupal 8 and hosted on enterprise platforms (Acquia/Pantheon), the member portal mirrors SmartLabs' operational structure:

Identity & Access Management

  • SSO authentication through company identity providers
  • Nightly LDAP synchronization for current user data
  • Role-based access with company-specific dashboards

Operational Features

  • Company admin tools for member management and badge requests
  • Integrated TEEM conference room scheduling
  • In-context Zendesk support (no tab-switching required)
  • Automated CRM field exports to HubSpot/Salesforce

Physical Access Control
We created a sophisticated badge management system with:

  • Unique badge entities with active/inactive states
  • Enforced company-specific lab and vivarium quotas
  • Automated email notifications for badge status changes
  • Clear UI feedback when limits are reached

3. Specialized Vivarium Portal
For organizations using In Vivo Research Suites, we developed a compliance-focused extension:

Digital Compliance Workflows

  • Online IACUC and IBC forms with versioning and attachments
  • Role-based approval processes that honor existing workflows
  • Paper-free compliance management

Advanced Scheduling & Integration

  • Booking system for procedure, imaging, surgical, and necropsy rooms
  • WiCom API integration for cage capacity data
  • BioRAFT safety training record synchronization
  • AAALAC-aligned access controls

Technical Architecture
The solution leverages a modular, multi-tenant architecture that supports scalable growth:

  • Frontend: Responsive design using Drupal's Layout Builder
  • Authentication: Enterprise SSO with LDAP synchronization
  • Hosting: Enterprise-grade platforms (Acquia/Pantheon)
  • Integrations: API connections to operational tools
  • Security: Role-based access with company-level data isolation

This modular approach means adding new locations, programs, or tools (like Stratocore for instrument scheduling) doesn't require system rebuilds.

Results & Impact
The integrated digital ecosystem delivers measurable benefits across all user groups:

For Marketing Teams

  • Streamlined content publishing workflow
  • Consistent brand presentation across all touchpoints
  • Clear conversion path from prospect to member

For Member Administrators

  • Single platform for staff onboarding, badge management, room booking, and compliance
  • Reduced administrative overhead through automation
  • Clear visibility into company resource usage and limits

For SmartLabs Leadership

  • Built-in governance through SSO and LDAP integration
  • Automated badge limit enforcement for physical security
  • Consolidated support and CRM data within operational workflows
  • Scalable architecture that grows with business expansion

For End Users

  • Intuitive, role-aware interfaces that surface relevant information
  • Seamless transitions between marketing, operational, and compliance functions
  • Reduced friction in daily workflows

Key Takeaways
This project demonstrates how thoughtful digital architecture can transform complex operational ecosystems into intuitive user experiences. By treating the platform as three interconnected but distinct experiences—marketing, operations, and compliance—we created a system that serves diverse user needs while maintaining operational coherence.

The SmartLabs digital ecosystem now enables the company to scale what differentiates them in the market: speed to science, operational reliability, and regulatory compliance. Most importantly, it removes digital friction so biotech teams can focus on what matters most—advancing scientific discovery.
