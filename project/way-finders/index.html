Way Finders: Reimagining a Community Portal

  • Budget: $50,000 - $100,000

Client Overview
Way Finders (formerly HAP Housing) is a leading community development organization serving Massachusetts, dedicated to creating pathways to affordable housing and community empowerment. As a respected member of the NeighborWorks network, Way Finders has built a prestigious reputation for innovative housing solutions, comprehensive community engagement, and measurable impact in addressing housing challenges across Western Massachusetts.

Project Challenge
Way Finders needed a complete digital transformation to support their organizational rebrand from HAP Housing while creating a unified web presence for their three distinct lines of business: Housing Center, Real Estate Development, and Community Engagement. The challenge was to build a cohesive digital ecosystem that could:

  • Support complex multi-business line navigation and content organization
  • Integrate with multiple third-party systems (Newton Software, Eventbrite, Blackbaud/eTapestry, GoSection8)
  • Provide multilingual accessibility through Google Translate integration
  • Deliver responsive experiences across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
  • Enable sophisticated content filtering and search capabilities

Technical Solution

Multi-Site Architecture on Single Codebase
We developed an innovative Drupal solution that presents four distinct site sections while maintaining a unified codebase, template system, and taxonomy structure:

  • Wayfinders (Main) - Primary organizational content
  • Housing Center - Individual and family housing services
  • Real Estate - Development and property management
  • Community Engagement - Collaborative programs and partnerships

Advanced Content Management System

Custom Content Types Developed:

  • Homepage with animated hero sections and promotional content blocks
  • Gateway pages for each business line with dynamic navigation
  • Property management system with multi-image galleries and filterable views
  • Team/Staff directory with hierarchical organization
  • Resource center with advanced filtering by subject matter and content type
  • News, Ideas & Impact hub with multi-select filtering capabilities

Sophisticated Taxonomy Implementation:

  • Line of Business categorization for content promotion and filtering
  • Position-based staff organization (Executive Board, Senior Team, Resource Development)
  • Multi-level content classification system for resources and impact materials
  • Subject matter categorization for housing-specific resources

Third-Party System Integrations

  • Newton Software: Career portal integration with custom CSS styling
  • Eventbrite: Real-time events feed for all organizational programming
  • Blackbaud/eTapestry: Donation processing and mailing list management via iframe embedding
  • GoSection8: Housing search functionality with responsive off-site linking
  • Google Translate: Multi-language support for 9 required languages (English, Arabic, Cambodian, Chinese, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Somali, Spanish, Vietnamese)
  • YouTube: Video embedding capabilities throughout the site

Dynamic Navigation System
We created an intelligent navigation system that adapts based on user location within the site:

  • Contextual Logo Changes: Logo updates when users enter specific business line sections
  • Collapsible Main Navigation: Primary navigation collapses to hamburger menu within business line sections
  • Active State Management: Secondary navigation highlights current business line with child links on hover
  • Right-Rail Menu Trees: Dynamic sidebar navigation showing current page hierarchy and related content

Advanced View and Filtering System

Custom Views Developed:

  • Properties View: Filterable by completion status with hover overlay details
  • Team/Staff Views: Organized by position and department with pagination
  • Resource Center: Dual-filter system by subject matter and content type
  • News/Ideas/Impact: Multi-select filtering with visual tile displays
  • Real Estate Development: Promoted properties with embedded video support
  • Property & Asset Management: Dedicated property showcase with team integration

Design and User Experience

Responsive Design Implementation
Working closely with TSM Design, we implemented a fully responsive experience with:

  • Desktop Optimization: Full-featured layouts with advanced navigation
  • Tablet Adaptation: Streamlined interfaces maintaining full functionality
  • Mobile Enhancement: Touch-optimized navigation with collapsible elements

Visual Elements and Animation

  • Hero Animation: Coordinated line-building animations on homepage scroll
  • Interactive Galleries: Scrollable partner/collaborator logo displays
  • Hover Effects: Property and team member image overlays with detailed information
  • Icon Integration: Animated iconography throughout promotional content sections

Technical Specifications

User Role Management
Implemented a four-tier user permission system:

  • Root/Admin: Full system access for technical administration
  • Editor: Complete content management with user administration capabilities
  • Author: Content creation and editing with publishing rights
  • Contributor: Content creation requiring editorial approval

Performance and Accessibility Features

  • Print Functionality: Custom print stylesheets for document generation
  • Search Optimization: Site-wide search including all content types and resources
  • CAPTCHA Integration: Enhanced form security with reCAPTCHA implementation
  • Cache Management: Editor-level cache clearing capabilities for content updates

Results and Impact

Organizational Transformation
The new website successfully supported Way Finders' rebrand while creating a sophisticated digital presence that effectively communicates their multi-faceted mission. The unified yet distinct business line approach allows visitors to easily navigate between services while maintaining awareness of the organization's comprehensive offerings.

Technical Excellence

  • Seamless Integration: All third-party systems integrated smoothly with custom styling
  • Content Management Efficiency: Editors can efficiently manage complex content across multiple business lines
  • Scalable Architecture: The flexible taxonomy and content type system supports future organizational growth
  • Multi-language Accessibility: Google Translate integration serves Way Finders' diverse community base

User Experience Enhancement

  • Intuitive Navigation: Users can easily transition between business lines while maintaining context
  • Rich Content Discovery: Advanced filtering systems help visitors find relevant resources and information
  • Mobile Optimization: Full functionality maintained across all device types
  • Community Engagement: Integrated events, news, and resource systems encourage ongoing visitor engagement

Conclusion
The Way Finders website project demonstrates the power of strategic technical architecture in supporting complex organizational needs. By leveraging Drupal's flexibility alongside custom development and thoughtful design implementation, we created a digital platform that not only serves Way Finders' current operational requirements but also provides a foundation for future growth and community impact.

The successful integration of multiple business lines, third-party systems, and user types within a single, maintainable codebase showcases the potential for sophisticated non-profit digital solutions that drive mission-critical outcomes.
