Client Background
The American Institute for Economic Research (AIER), founded in 1933 by economist and financial advisor Colonel Edward C. Harwood, is the oldest economic research institute in the United States. AIER is a nonpartisan research and education nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization focused on the importance of markets, educating people on the value of personal freedom, free enterprise, property rights, limited government, and sound money. Based in Great Barrington, Massachusetts, AIER has maintained its prestigious position in economic research for over 90 years, with scholars regularly contributing to major publications and media outlets.

Project Goals & Challenges

Primary Objectives

  • Legacy System Migration: Transition from outdated Drupal 6 to modern Drupal 7 platform
  • Content Consolidation: Streamline multiple content types and eliminate redundant workflows
  • Enhanced User Experience: Implement responsive design with mobile-first approach
  • Content Management Efficiency: Simplify editorial workflows for AIER staff
  • Performance Optimization: Improve site speed and search functionality

Key Challenges

  • Complex Content Architecture: Managing diverse content types including research papers, news articles, educational materials, and multimedia content
  • Workflow Simplification: Consolidating the existing dual-node system (article + bucket nodes) for homepage features
  • Mobile Responsiveness: Ensuring optimal experience across all devices for academic and general audiences
  • SEO & Archival Needs: Maintaining research document accessibility while implementing 12-month display cycles

The project's success lies in its balanced approach to technical innovation and practical usability, ensuring that AIER's prestigious research and educational content remains accessible and engaging across all digital platforms while providing the institutional flexibility needed for ongoing content management and growth.
