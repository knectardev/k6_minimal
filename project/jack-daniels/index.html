Introduction to the Client
Jack Daniel's is one of the world's best‑known whiskey brands. The brand is produced at the Jack Daniel Distillery in Lynchburg, Tennessee and has been owned by the Brown–Forman Corporation since 1956. Jack Daniel's Tennessee whiskey is packaged in distinctive square bottles and sold on a massive scale; its "Black Label" expression alone sold 12.9 million nine‑litre cases in 2017, and the broader family of products—Tennessee Honey, Tennessee Apple, Gentleman Jack, Tennessee Fire and ready‑to‑drink variations—has generated more than 16.1 million equivalent casesen.wikipedia.org. The distillery itself is the oldest registered distillery in the United Statesglobenewswire.com, giving the brand an historic prestige that few spirits can match.

Project Background and Partners
By 2012 Jack Daniel's needed a modern digital experience that honoured its heritage while meeting the expectations of a mobile audience. The company partnered with Arnold Worldwide—an advertising agency founded in 1946 and now part of the Havas Creative Group—to create the new jackdaniels.com. Arnold is a global communications agency that has been one of the most creatively awarded agencies of the past decadeglobenewswire.com, serving clients ranging from Progressive Insurance and Sam's Club to McDonald's and Volvoglobenewswire.com. Knectar was engaged to provide technical consultation, development and back‑end Drupal architecture while Arnold's front‑end team handled the design and user‑interface developmentknectar.com.

A key goal of the project was to create a responsively designed website that adapts to desktops, tablets and mobile devicesglobenewswire.com. The site needed to provide a global experience—serving up localised content to visitors from different countries—while telling the brand's 137‑year story through multimediaglobenewswire.com. In addition to rich storytelling, Jack Daniel's wanted to offer product information, distillery‑visit details, recipes and e‑commerce links in a single, cohesive experienceglobenewswire.com.

Challenges

  • Legal compliance and localisation: As an alcoholic‑beverage brand, Jack Daniel's had to verify a visitor's legal drinking age and comply with differing age requirements across markets. It also had to tailor content (language, privacy notices and available products) based on the visitor's country.
  • Rich storytelling: The client wanted an engaging way to present its long history and unique culture using multimedia while keeping the site manageable for content editors.
  • Product and registration workflows: Beyond brand storytelling, the site needed to provide detailed product information, allow enthusiasts to register special bottles and encourage exploration of cocktail recipes and experiences.
  • Technical integration: The site would run on a Windows Server/IIS/MS SQL stack, and it needed to consume Brown‑Forman's SOAP‑based web services for tasks such as age verification and form submissionsknectar.com. Drupal had to be tailored to this environment while remaining performant and secure.

Solution

Partnership and Agile Delivery
Knectar and Arnold adopted an agile approach that allowed the team to move from concept to launch in only 15 weeksglobenewswire.com. Arnold's designers delivered client‑facing code to a shared repository while Knectar focused on back‑end architecture, integrating Drupal with external web services and ensuring the site's responsive behaviourknectar.com. This collaboration allowed creative and technical teams to iterate quickly without blocking one another.

Age Gate and Country‑specific Content
The site opens with an age‑verification gate. Visitors select their country and language and enter their birth date; a web‑service call (ValidateLDA) checks the legal drinking age for the selected country and denies access if the visitor is underage. The system stores the visitor's country in a session cookie for two hours, enabling Drupal to filter content by country. Localised footers, privacy statements, terms of use and feature content are displayed according to the selected country, and the age gate can toggle features such as cookie tick boxes for jurisdictions like the UK and Ireland.

Multimedia Timeline
To tell the brand's history, the team built a vertical "Our Story" timeline. A feature content type supports a multimedia slideshow at the top, with fields for titles, up to four images or videos, captions and call‑to‑action buttons. Below the feature, the timeline includes stamps and spotlights. Stamps display circular thumbnails on the left and hold fields for images, titles, descriptions and multiple calls to action. Spotlights appear on the right and have fields for number images, titles, descriptions and media galleries. Content editors can control the order of timeline items through weight fields and promote specific items to the top. When visitors access the site, they are greeted by this scrolling multimedia timeline that mixes videos, photos, product information, company news and historical factsglobenewswire.com. Facebook "Like" and Comment widgets integrate social engagement into spotlight details.

Product Gateway and Detail Pages
The product gateway showcases Jack Daniel's four primary product lines—Gentleman Jack, Tennessee Honey, Old No. 7 and Single Barrel—and provides entry points to detailed pages for each. Drupal's content types were used to manage both primary and secondary products. Primary detail pages feature a WYSIWYG‑controlled header for custom imagery and text, a media gallery, related recipes and cross‑links to other products. Secondary products are presented as a list with mouse‑over views and fields for titles, volumes, call‑to‑action links and publish dates.

Register‑Your‑Bottle Workflow
Enthusiasts can register commemorative bottles via a detailed form. Users first select a product and then enter information such as bottle number, personal details (name, address, birth date, email), and contact preferences. The form also includes survey questions about how often visitors drink Jack Daniel's and their likelihood of recommending the brand. Drupal captures the data and passes it to Brown‑Forman's CRM via a web‑service call. This workflow allows the brand to build a global consumer database while respecting regional opt‑in preferences.

Recipes, Experiences and Visit Us
Separate content types manage recipes and experiences. Recipe nodes include fields for titles, images, ingredient lists and instructions, while experience nodes have titles, images, descriptions and call‑to‑action links. Both content types support country tags so that only relevant items appear based on the visitor's age‑gate selection. The "Visit Us" page and the privacy and registration pages are managed through WYSIWYG fields, enabling editors to update text and imagery without developer intervention.

Technical Details
The project used Drupal 7 as the CMS and was deployed on a Windows Server 2008 R2/IIS 7.5 environment with an MS SQL 2005 database and PHP 5.3 via FastCGI. Although Drupal is more commonly hosted on Linux/Apache stacks, the team replicated the production environment in development to surface and resolve compatibility issues. Advanced SOAP web‑service connectors were developed to integrate with Brown‑Forman's systems for age verification, country lists and registration dataknectar.com. The responsive front‑end was built using HTML5 and CSS3 so that the same codebase adapts gracefully to desktops, tablets and mobile devicesglobenewswire.com.

Results and Impact

  • Global reach: The new jackdaniels.com launched in June 2012 as the brand's first responsive site and quickly rolled out to 185 countries with seven English‑language versions and plans for localisationglobenewswire.com. Country‑specific cookies and content tagging allowed the brand to tailor the experience to local regulations and preferences without duplicating the entire site.
  • Rich brand storytelling: The immersive timeline, product galleries and recipe/experience sections gave visitors an insider's look at Jack Daniel's history and culture. The combination of multimedia storytelling and social‑media integration encouraged deeper engagementglobenewswire.com.
  • Compliance and data capture: The age gate and bottle‑registration workflow ensured legal compliance and enabled Jack Daniel's to collect opt‑in consumer data across markets. Server‑side validation and web‑service integration reduced errors and manual work.
  • Agile delivery and maintainability: Thanks to the agile partnership between Arnold and Knectar, the project was completed from concept to launch in only 15 weeksglobenewswire.com. The use of Drupal content types and WYSIWYG fields empowers content editors to manage and update the site without ongoing developer involvement.

Conclusion
Jack Daniel's global website exemplifies how thoughtful collaboration between creative and technical teams can translate a storied heritage into a modern digital experience. By combining Arnold Worldwide's brand storytelling and design expertise with Knectar's enterprise Drupal development, the project delivered a responsive, legally compliant and globally localised platform that honours Jack Daniel's past while serving the needs of today's connected consumers. Appendix: Site Feature Summary
The key functional areas of the site are summarised below. Each item lists its purpose and highlights the Drupal implementation that supported it:

  • Age Gate – verifies a visitor's legal drinking age and stores their country selection. A call to the ValidateLDA web service compares the entered birth date against the legal age for the selected country and, if approved, Drupal stores the country in a session cookie. This cookie drives localisation of footers, terms and other content.
  • Timeline – presents the brand's history through a vertical timeline of multimedia content. The timeline uses three Drupal content types: a feature type for a slideshow at the top, stamps that appear on the left with images and descriptions, and spotlights that appear on the right with number images, descriptions and media galleries.
  • Product Gateway – showcases Jack Daniel's product lines and links to detail pages. Primary and secondary product content types manage custom headers, media galleries and cross‑links to related products.
  • Bottle Registration – collects data from consumers who register commemorative bottles. The Drupal form includes fields for bottle number, personal information and preferences, then posts the data to Brown‑Forman's CRM via a web‑service call.
  • Recipes & Experiences – inspire enthusiasts with cocktail recipes and brand experiences. Separate content types hold titles, images, ingredient lists and instructions; country tags ensure that only relevant items appear.
  • Visit Us & Privacy – provide distillery information and legal notices. These pages use WYSIWYG fields so content managers can update text and images easily, and multiple versions can be created for different countries.
