Knectar | Web Development & Digital Solutions

Knectar is a strategic web development company founded by Chris Amato in 2005, operating as a central node in the network of digital product creation. Based in Springfield, Massachusetts with reach to Boston, New York, and Chicago, we specialize in sophisticated web solutions for world-class brands, universities, and startups.

What We Do

We operate at the intersection of vision, execution, and connection — where product design, engineering, and user needs converge. Our expertise spans custom website development, API integrations, e-commerce solutions, and complex web architecture. We bridge disciplines, align teams, and translate technical complexity into user-centered clarity.

Chris Amato, CEO, Senior Product & Project Manager

Chris Amato is the Owner and Senior Product/Project Manager at Knectar, where he integrates strategic vision, technical expertise, and design thinking to deliver impactful, user-centered solutions. With deep experience in product strategy, Chris leads teams through empathetic user research, prioritization frameworks, and clear roadmap definitions, ensuring alignment with strategic objectives and measurable outcomes.

Chris adeptly manages technical complexities across open-source ecosystems such as Drupal, WordPress and Django, emphasizing quality through test-driven development, detailed documentation, and rigorous QA automation. His innovative approach extends to leveraging advanced AI technologies like GPT-4 and Cursor, as well as physical prototyping using tools such as Rhino3D and CNC, consistently producing thoughtful and market-ready solutions.
